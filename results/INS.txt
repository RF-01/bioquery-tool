INTERPRETIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Insulin (INS, Homo sapiens) is a protein of 110 amino acids. Function: Insulin decreases blood glucose concentration. It increases cell permeability to monosaccharides, amino acids and fatty acids. It accelerates glycolysis, the pentose phosphate cycle, and glycogen synthesis in liver Localized to: Secreted. Top biological processes: G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway; acute-phase response; alpha-beta T cell activation; fatty acid homeostasis; glucose homeostasis. Top molecular functions: hormone activity; identical protein binding; insulin receptor binding; insulin-like growth factor receptor binding; protease binding. Cellular components: extracellular space; Golgi lumen; Golgi membrane; endoplasmic reticulum lumen; endoplasmic reticulum-Golgi intermediate compartment membrane. Participates in KEGG pathways including: MAPK signaling pathway; Ras signaling pathway; Rap1 signaling pathway; cGMP-PKG signaling pathway; HIF-1 signaling pathway; FoxO signaling pathway.
